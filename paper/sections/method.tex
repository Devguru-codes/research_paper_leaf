\section{Models}
\label{sec:method}

\subsection{A ladder of matched models}
\label{sec:controls}
To isolate what matters, we compare seven models that share the backbone, a light vein head on early
backbone features with its auxiliary loss, the training recipe and, where applicable, the token width
(192) and transformer depth (2). Each step adds one ingredient; all but the first concatenate their
output with the backbone's global token before the linear classifier.
\begin{enumerate}
 \item \textbf{plain}: backbone global token only, i.e.\ top-stage features averaged over the whole
   image, background included;
 \item \textbf{patch pooling}: adds a branch on the mid-level (stage-3) patch tokens, an MLP per token
   averaged over the whole image; the extra features and capacity that all following models share,
   without any mask;
 \item \textbf{mask pooling}: the same branch averaged over the leaf mask only; the model knows
   \emph{where} the leaf is but pools it as a whole;
 \item \textbf{strip pooling}~\citep{hou2020strip}: mask-weighted means of the patch tokens over every
   row and column of the patch grid, with learned positions and the transformer; localised pooling along
   the image axes;
 \item \textbf{slab-only}: \SVA{} (\cref{sec:sva}) without measurements, so each token only pools the
   patches of its slab of an object-centred directional sweep;
 \item \textbf{\SVA{}}: slab tokens that also carry measured leaf geometry;
 \item \textbf{global fusion}: the full grid of \SVA{}'s measurements flattened through an MLP and fused
   globally (in the spirit of~\citep{phgnet2023,gu2026multifiltration}); the same measurements without
   localised pooling.
\end{enumerate}
The contrasts are: 2 vs.\ 1 (\emph{mid-level features}); 3 vs.\ 2 (\emph{leaf location}); 4, 5 and 6
vs.\ 3 (\emph{localisation}); 6 vs.\ 5 and 6 vs.\ 4 (\emph{measured geometry}, and object-centred slabs
vs.\ image-axis strips); and 7 vs.\ 1 (\emph{geometry without localisation}). Every model receives the
same field crops, centred by our masker (\cref{sec:data}); models 3--7 additionally use the predicted
mask inside the network, whereas models 1 and 2 use only the crop. The masker is a second network that
costs about as much as the classifier (\cref{app:cost}).

\subsection{Sweep-valuation attention}
\label{sec:sva}
\SVA{} is our attempt to give a network explicit, local leaf geometry (\cref{fig:overview}). Two
structural fields are derived from the image: the \emph{blade} (the leaf mask $m$) and a \emph{learned
venation field} $v$, the vein probability predicted by the vein head, which is pseudo-supervised by a
multiscale Sato ridge response~\citep{sato1998ridge} and is not an observed vascular structure. On a
$96\times96$ grid in the blade's centroid/RMS frame, each field becomes a weighted cubical complex whose
cell weights are conditioned on appearance: per-pixel weights $a_{\mathrm b}=m\,\sigma(g_{\mathrm b}(f))$
and $a_{\mathrm v}=m\,v\,\sigma(g_{\mathrm v}(f))$ from mid-level features $f$ and small MLPs $g$, and
each edge and square inherits the minimum weight of its pixels,
\begin{equation}
  w_0(p)=a(p),\qquad w_1(e)=\min_{v\in e}a(v),\qquad w_2(q)=\min_{v\in q}a(v),
  \label{eq:weights}
\end{equation}
which keeps the weighted complex a fuzzy subcomplex and reduces to the ordinary cubical complex for
binary weights. With $H_d$ the weighted number of $d$-cells, area $A=H_2$, boundary length
$P=2H_1-4H_2$ and Euler characteristic $\chi=H_0-H_1+H_2$ are the digital intrinsic volumes
(\cref{app:sva}).

For each of $K{=}32$ directions $\theta$, the sweep is cut into $T{=}16$ soft slabs with the sigmoid
relaxation of DECT~\citep{roell2024dect}. For every slab, \SVA{} records how much area and boundary
length of both fields enter it (and how much has entered so far), which describes the local width and
boundary complexity of the leaf at that position. Each slab yields one token whose query is built from
these measurements and which attends only to the image patches of the same slab; the 512 tokens are
mixed by a two-layer transformer and mean-pooled. To the best of our knowledge, no prior work
co-registers filtration-derived valuation increments with attention restricted to the corresponding
filtration slab (\cref{sec:related}); our experiments show, however, that neither the measurements nor
the restriction is what makes the model transfer. The equations, the efficient histogram
computation, the Euler and Hadwiger token variants and the rotation property are given in
\cref{app:sva}.
